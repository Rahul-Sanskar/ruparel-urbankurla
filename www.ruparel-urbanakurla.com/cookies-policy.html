A cookie is a small piece of data that a website sends to your browser when you access information at that site. A cookie is stored as a simple text file on your computer or mobile device by a website's server and only that server will be able to retrieve or read the contents of that cookie. Cookies let you navigate between pages efficiently as they store your preferences, and generally improve your experience of a website. www.ruparel-urbanakurla.com uses the following types of cookies to enhance your experience and interactivity:

Analytics cookies

Used for anonymously remembering your computer or mobile device when you visit our website to keep track of browsing patterns and advertising performance.

Service cookies

Used to make our website work efficiently, for example to keep your enquiry session secure and remember your settings and the pages you view.

Per-session cookies

Non-persistent cookies that serve technical purposes of providing seamless navigation. They are deleted as soon as you close your browser.
