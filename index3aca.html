Welcome to RUPAREL ESTATES INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED

RUPAREL ESTATES INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED is a premium residential address off the Eastern Express Highway in Kurla. This stunning community comprises 1 and 2 BHK apartments with balconies. The apartments feature an efficient layout for optimal space utilisation. The apartments are designed with premium finishes and branded sanitary fittings. Moreover, it hosts a range of amenities including a rooftop pool with a deck, senior citizen pergola, skywalk, chess zone, multipurpose sports court, open theatre, kids play area, party lawn, etc.

The rising locality of Central Suburbs provides some of the finest social facilities. Your property is only a few distance from Dhirubhai Ambani International School, Mt. Litera School, SIES College, KJ Somaiya College, Asian Heart Institute, Surana Sethia Hospital, Jio World Drive, Phoenix Marketcity and BKC. So, lead an exceptional lifestyle at RUPAREL ESTATES INDIA PRIVATE LIMITED in the centralised location of Mumbai.
